## Data, purposes and legal bases

We process the minimum business identity, employer, role, contact and account data needed to create
accounts, authenticate users, administer subscriptions and prevent abuse. We process customer alert
preferences, Telegram/chat identifiers, email addresses, WhatsApp numbers and delivery receipts to
deliver and troubleshoot requested alerts. We process access, approval, correction, audit, security
and error metadata to operate, secure and evidence the service. Support messages are used to answer
requests and improve operations.

Counsel must confirm and document the applicable basis for each purpose. The intended bases are
performance of the customer contract where applicable and legitimate interests in operating a
secure B2B intelligence service; consent must be recorded where law or channel rules require it,
including WhatsApp opt-in. We do not use customer contacts for unrelated marketing and do not put
tracking pixels in intelligence briefs.

## Intelligence sources and AI

The platform collects only sources approved in the source-rights register. Public reporting may
incidentally contain names, titles or professional contact details. Collection must be necessary,
proportionate and consistent with the approved source terms. AI produces shadow extraction
proposals; it has no publication authority. A named human reviews every release. The service does
not use customer data to make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant
effects.

## Recipients, subprocessors and transfers

Access is limited to authorised staff and contracted processors needed for hosting, authentication,
AI processing, delivery, monitoring and support. The current proposed list is maintained in
`subprocessor-register.md`. Counsel must verify each processor agreement, data location, deletion
terms and any transfer mechanism before activation. We disclose information where legally required
or needed to protect users and the service, with documented approval.

## Retention and security

Account and delivery records are kept for [period] after the customer relationship; security and

Depending on applicable law, individuals may request access, correction, deletion, restriction,
portability or objection, and may withdraw consent without affecting earlier processing. Requests go
to [privacy email]; identity will be proportionately verified. Individuals may complain to their
competent supervisory authority, including [lead authority and link].
