# Source-rights decision register

**DRAFT — a source remains disabled unless a named authorised reviewer records an approval.**

The technical catalogue is `config/source_candidates.json`; the latest non-destructive preflight is
`outputs/source-preflight-2026-07-21.json`. Technical health, public accessibility and robots
allowance are not licences and do not establish collection, storage, AI-processing or republication
rights.

For every source, counsel/licensing owner must record: source and legal entity; evidence URL and
captured terms version/date; access method; applicable database/copyright/contract restrictions;
robots result; permitted frequency; permitted stored fields and retention; quotation/attribution;
customer-display and redistribution scope; AI/provider processing permission; geography; personal
data basis; contact/permission correspondence; decision (`approved`, `approved-with-conditions` or
`rejected`); conditions; reviewer name; review/expiry dates; and immutable evidence ID.

Approval must be translated back into a separately reviewed activation change. The platform must
not activate a row merely because this document or a candidate entry exists. A material terms,
endpoint, ownership or product-use change reopens review and disables collection where necessary.
