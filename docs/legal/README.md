# Legal and commercial launch pack

**Status: DRAFT — not legal advice and not approved for signature or publication.**

These product-specific drafts give external counsel and the company owner a concrete review set.
Bracketed fields must be completed, negotiated clauses must be selected, and counsel must record a
dated approval. No filename or checklist item is evidence that a source licence, contract,
insurance policy or data-processing agreement exists.

The pack contains the privacy notice, service terms, Article 28 processor addendum, customer order
form, subprocessor register, insurance submission and source-rights decision register. The company
must also populate `config/operational_contacts.example.yaml` with real accountable people.

The drafts assume a B2B maritime intelligence support service with human editorial approval. They
must be reconsidered if the product begins automated decision-making, vessel tracking, sanctions
screening, consumer sales, navigational advice, or processing special-category/personal location
data.
