# B2B service terms — counsel draft

**DRAFT — do not offer or sign until qualified counsel approves every bracketed or negotiated term.**

## 1. Parties, order and service

These terms govern the order between [LEGAL COMPANY NAME] ("Supplier") and the business customer
identified in the order form. Supplier provides a human-reviewed maritime event intelligence and
delivery service for the agreed users, regions and channels. Order form, these terms, DPA and any
service schedule form the agreement; the negotiated precedence is [insert].

## 2. Intended use and critical limitation

The service is decision support. It is not a navigation system, voyage instruction, security or
military advice, legal or sanctions opinion, emergency service, or substitute for official notices,
masters, flag/coastal authorities, insurers, counsel or the customer's own risk processes. Customer
must independently verify information before acting and must not configure the service as the sole
control for safety-critical or legally significant decisions.

Reports distinguish confirmed, reported and unknown information. Public and third-party sources may
be late, incomplete, contradictory or wrong. Supplier will use the agreed editorial process but does
not promise complete event coverage, uninterrupted delivery, a particular operational outcome or
that every error will be identified before publication.

## 3. Human editorial control and corrections

AI may assist with shadow extraction and prioritisation but may not publish. A named human approves
every release. Supplier records versions, sources, approvals and corrections and will issue a
correction through affected channels under the incident procedure. Customer must promptly report
suspected errors to [editorial escalation contact].

## 4. Customer responsibilities

Customer will provide accurate authorised contacts, protect credentials, enforce least privilege,
maintain alternate emergency channels, test delivery and notify Supplier of personnel changes. It
will obtain recipient/channel consent, including valid WhatsApp opt-in; follow platform terms; and
not resell, scrape, reverse engineer, remove attribution, republish source material, train competing
models on outputs, overload the service or use it unlawfully. Permitted internal distribution is
limited to [named group/affiliates].

## 5. Sources, intellectual property and feedback

Supplier retains the platform, editorial structure and original analyses. Customer retains its data.
Third-party source material remains owned by its licensors and is subject to attribution, quotation,
retention and republication limits in the source register. No licence to underlying source databases
is granted. Customer receives a [non-exclusive, non-transferable] subscription licence for the term.

## 6. Security, privacy and confidentiality

Each party will protect confidential information using reasonable controls. Supplier will maintain
the documented security measures, incident contacts and DPA. Security incidents affecting customer
personal data will be notified without undue delay and within any negotiated period [hours], subject
to verified facts and lawful restrictions. Required disclosure will be limited and, where lawful,
notified.

## 7. Availability, support and change control

Support hours, severity definitions, target response times, maintenance, delivery objectives and
service credits (if any) are only those in the order form. Delivery acceptance by a third-party
provider is not proof that the human recipient read a message. Material changes to sources,
processors, model systems or publication controls follow the documented change procedure.

## 8. Fees, term, suspension and exit

Fees, taxes, payment, renewal and term are in the order form. Supplier may immediately use a scoped
kill switch or suspend access for a security threat, unlawful use, non-payment or channel/provider
requirement, and will restore service when safe. On termination, access ends and customer data is
returned/deleted under the DPA, subject to legal holds and immutable records required by law.

## 9. Warranties, liability and indemnities

Each party warrants authority to contract. All other warranties and the proposed liability cap,
excluded losses, carve-outs, customer misuse indemnity, IP indemnity and insurance obligations must
be drafted and approved by counsel for the governing law and risk profile. **No default liability
cap is supplied here because an arbitrary number would create false protection.**

## 10. General

Counsel must complete governing law, courts/arbitration, notices, assignment, subcontracting,
force majeure, export/sanctions compliance, anti-bribery, entire agreement, waiver, severability and
survival. Signatures belong in the approved order form, not this draft.
