# Professional indemnity / E&O broker submission

**DRAFT — this is a risk questionnaire, not insurance and not evidence of cover.**

## Business description

[LEGAL COMPANY NAME] proposes a B2B maritime event intelligence support service covering the East
Mediterranean. It monitors approved public/licensed sources, preserves source evidence, separates
confirmed/reported/unknown claims and sends human-approved updates and corrections. It is not a
navigational, emergency, military, legal or sanctions-advice service. AI proposes claim extraction
in shadow mode and cannot publish.

## Controls to disclose accurately

- named senior human approval for every release; no autonomous publishing;
- two separate MFA-protected operators and least-privilege roles;
- source-rights register and collection disabled until approval;
- versioned evidence, approvals, customer delivery and correction receipts;
- outbound and per-channel kill switches, retries and duplicate prevention;
- private encrypted infrastructure, managed secrets, backups/restore drills and monitoring;
- customer contracts prohibit sole safety-critical reliance and require alternate official channels;
- documented security, privacy, incident, editorial and escalation procedures;
- 60-day multilingual shadow evaluation with precision, recall, hallucination and miss measures.

## Broker questions and evidence

Request quotations for technology E&O/professional indemnity, cyber/privacy, media liability and any
marine-professional exposure the broker identifies. Provide forecast revenue, territories,
customer types, largest contract, limits/retention sought, retroactive date, prior work/claims,
subcontractors, data volumes, source/licence status, service levels, limitation/indemnity language,
security testing and incident history. Ask specifically whether inaccurate/late intelligence,
failure to alert, content/IP claims, AI-assisted work, sanctions-related allegations, defence costs,
regulatory investigation, breach response and worldwide claims are included or excluded.

Do not launch paid real-time alerts until the authorised owner receives the bound policy, wording,
schedule, endorsements, receipt and broker confirmation; counsel confirms contractual limits align;
and policy number, limits, exclusions, renewal/notification owners and evidence location are entered
in the launch record.
